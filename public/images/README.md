# Profile Image

Place your profile photo here as `profile.jpg`.

## Requirements

- **File name**: `profile.jpg` (or `profile.png`)
- **Recommended size**: 800x800px (square)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Quality**: High resolution for best results
- **Style**: Professional headshot or portrait

## Tips

1. **Square crop**: Make sure your image is square (1:1 aspect ratio)
2. **Centered face**: Position your face in the center of the frame
3. **Good lighting**: Use well-lit photos for professional appearance
4. **Clean background**: Solid or blurred backgrounds work best
5. **File size**: Keep under 1MB for optimal loading

## Current Setup

The image will appear:
- ✅ Circular shape with border
- ✅ Responsive sizing (256px to 384px)
- ✅ Animated glow effect
- ✅ Left side on desktop, centered on mobile

If you don't have a photo ready, you can:
1. Use a professional avatar generator
2. Use an AI-generated portrait
3. Create a logo or initials-based image
